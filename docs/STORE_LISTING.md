# Store listing text

**App name:** SafeCopy

**Short description (≤ 80 chars):**
Mask your Aadhaar and PAN before sharing. Works fully offline.

**Full description:**

Someone asked for a copy of your Aadhaar or PAN? Send a safer one.

SafeCopy works fully offline. It has no internet permission, so your ID photos never leave your phone.

What it does:
• Finds your Aadhaar number, VID, PAN and QR code and covers them with solid black boxes. The last 4 digits of your Aadhaar stay visible, the way a masked Aadhaar does.
• Stamps who the copy is for, why, and the date across the whole image, so a copy given to a hotel can't easily be passed off as one given to a bank.
• Saves a brand-new image with no hidden location, phone model or date information.
• Lets you draw extra boxes over anything else, such as your photo, address or signature.

How to use it from WhatsApp: open the ID photo, tap Share, choose SafeCopy, then share the safe copy back.

What it doesn't do: SafeCopy is not an official app and isn't affiliated with UIDAI or the Income Tax Department. It doesn't make a document valid or invalid, and some organisations may still need the full document. The watermark discourages misuse but can't make a copy impossible to edit.

No accounts. No ads. No analytics. No data collected.
